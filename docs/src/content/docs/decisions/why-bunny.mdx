---
title: Why use Bunny.net?
description: Answering why I use Bunny.net.
---
I use [Bunny.net](https://bunny.net) as the <abbr title="Content Delivery Network">CDN</abbr> for my media files.

There are plenty of other CDN providers to choose from.

I chose Bunny because they're European.

## Sovereignty

Where possible I use European infrastructure.

It's closer to home, physically, legally, and ethically.

## That's it

Right now that's enough for me to choose Bunny.

I have only just started using them, so I will come back one day to expand on this.

I could have picked from a number of providers, but Bunny has been recommended by lots of IndieWeb neighbours, even those who live [outside of Europe](https://burgeonlab.com/blog/hosting-static-sites-with-github-pages-or-bunny-net/).
